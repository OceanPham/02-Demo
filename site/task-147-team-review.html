Decisions to preserve

  1. Keep four quantities distinct: actual valid work, approved payable time, checked client-billable time and final invoiced quantity.
  2. NGOCSON approves compensation/client billability; Daniel sets final invoice quantity, selling rates, amounts and currency, with a documented variance where applicable.
  3. Resolve the agreed freelancer cost rate effective on the work date; Work Type default is an estimate only. Missing/unsupported facts remain visible.
  4. Full work reports and freelancer costs stay internal. Optional client time breakdown reconciles exactly to final invoice lines.
  5. Phase 1 adds an internal report/export for manual freelancer-invoice reconciliation; no automatic payment, invoice matching or historical migration.

Verified current-system findings

  1. Compensation data absent

    TimeEntry has one actual duration, operational status, billable boolean and invoice pointers; no independent payable/billable minutes or compensation decision.

  2. Manual team process unverified

    Search of app entities/functions/components/helpers found generic supplier obligations and time exports, but no dedicated payable-hour reconciliation. The user has no current process example. Code cannot prove how the team works outside WORK.

  3. Costs disagree by consumer

    Standalone/Margin reports use snapshot then current-person fallback; Project Financials splits by operational approval; KPI/Queue use snapshot or zero; invoice-attached builder uses current User cost. Neither source nor historical effectiveness is consistently recorded.

  4. Cost/sale crossover

    WP hourly Apply and Create from Time can price sales using hourly_rate_snapshot. This must be removed from every hourly path, not only from one button.

  5. Actual time versus finance

    Task summary currently filters approved+billable. A financial correction today would have to alter duration; a separate financial decision/correction layer is needed.

  6. Invoice breakdown and frozen history

    Client attachment reuses work report calculations; front/server snapshot builders are duplicated. Both must reconcile final invoiced quantity while preserving old archived documents.

  7. Currency boundary

    Project schema offers EUR/USD/GBP, but User costs, PaymentObligation, margin formatter and issuance snapshot use EUR. A selectable invoice currency without an agreed conversion basis would make margins incomparable.

Shared reconciliation rules

  1. Worked-time inclusion

    Count saved, non-provisional valid registrations (pending/approved currently). Exclude invalid Rejected and Cancelled records from valid-work totals; retain them in review/audit. Keep signed factual corrections linked to the original.

  2. Financial decisions

    Compensation and client billing each have proposed minutes, reviewed approved minutes, decision state, actor, timestamp, reason/evidence and revision. Zero is a reviewed decision; null is unresolved. Each approved amount is independently 0..valid worked minutes.

  3. Disjoint hour buckets

    Actual valid minutes = payable + non-payable + pending compensation + unknown historical compensation. Independently actual valid minutes = reviewed client-billable + reviewed non-billable + pending/unknown billing minutes. Do not add compensation and billing columns together.

  4. Confirmed cost

    Reviewed payable minutes x verified agreed rate effective on work date. Reviewed non-payable minutes cost zero even when the rate is missing. Approval never marks a freelancer as paid.

  5. Estimated cost

    A separately labelled estimate for awaiting-review proposed hours with a known rate, or payable hours priced with a Work Type default. It never becomes confirmed cost. An estimate must identify its basis/as-of date.

  6. Unknown cost

    Expose affected hours/entries when compensation or applicable rate cannot be verified. Do not represent an unquantifiable cost as EUR 0 or a final margin. An optional scenario estimate must stay separate.

  7. Revenue and invoice facts

    Actual revenue follows eligible issued invoice lines and financial corrections, excluding VAT. Checked billable hours are a proposal; Daniel's documented final quantity is a separate fact. Fixed-price agreed/forecast value stays separate from issued revenue.

  8. Margin and forecast

    Confirmed-only margin must not be presented as the final outcome when unresolved costs remain. Show known confirmed costs, estimated exposure and unknown-cost counts/hours separately; projected margin is provisional. Total project cost can also include expenses/subscriptions without double counting supplier payments.

  9. Scope and periods

    Group manual reconciliation by stable freelancer ID and selected inclusive work-date interval; split rate bands across effective dates. Invoice issue/payment dates, lifetime project totals and filtered work-date reports can differ legitimately.

  10. Historical basis

    Preserve legacy client billable classification and invoice ownership; existing time approval or non-billable flag never proves compensation. Preserve issued snapshots and old exports; corrected analysis shows the new as-of/revision and evidence.

Proposed workflow and responsibilities

  1. 1. Register actual work

  2. 2. Check work and exceptions

Recommended technical design — proposal, not deployed schema

  1. Keep TimeEntry as work evidence

    Do not replace duration_minutes with payable or billable minutes. Keep start/end/date/project/task/WP and operational validity. Once financially processed, use linked factual corrections for actual-time errors.

  2. Append financial decision events

    Proposed separate decision ledger per time entry and decision kind, with proposed/approved minutes, pending/approved/unknown state, expected revision, idempotency key, actor/time/reason/evidence and predecessor. A read projection may expose latest values; exact schema names are proposals for review.

  3. Record date-effective freelancer agreements

    Proposed internal rate agreement history: freelancer ID, valid-from/to, rate/currency, evidence and approval. Resolve by work date; ambiguous/overlapping/missing agreements do not auto-confirm. Existing snapshots need provenance review, not blind promotion.

  4. One canonical projection

    A shared pure calculator takes time validity, latest decisions, rate evidence, scope and invoice allocations and returns actual/compensation/billing buckets plus confirmed/estimated/unknown costs. Frontend, backend readers, exports and caches use the same semantics.

  5. Authoritative write commands

    Server validates configured NGOCSON identity, active account, financial permission, row scope, integer bounds, revision and processed-record rules. Cost visibility uses existing financial access. Direct entity writes cannot change protected financial decisions. Do not infer identity from display name.

  6. Distinct invoice allocation

    Freeze checked source minutes, final invoice quantity/rate/currency, source entry IDs and documented variance. For a multi-row client breakdown use explicit billed allocations/adjustment rows, not altered actual registrations or fabricated timestamps. Verify sum equals final hourly line quantity before issue/send.

  7. Keep payment outside approval

    Phase 1 produces a reconciliation dataset for manual checking. Existing PaymentObligation may record a supplier invoice/payment independently, but is not evidence of approved hours and must not duplicate labour cost in project margin.

Simpler alternatives considered

  1. Two extra booleans

    Rejected as insufficient — Cannot represent partial amounts, pending/unknown values, date-effective rate evidence or independent audited corrections.

  2. Split/shorten time entries to match pay or billing

    Rejected — Would lose actual effort or fragment one registration; financial decisions would change the underlying work evidence.

  3. Minimal fields on TimeEntry + AuditEvent

    Simpler implementation option — Separate minute/status fields and robust immutable audit may suffice if concurrency and processed financial-only corrections are proven. Compare to the proposed ledger at team review; direct unguarded field edits are unacceptable.

  4. Separate decision ledger + shared projection

    Recommended proposal — Preserves one registration, independent decisions and review/correction history. Requires explicit server commands, projection completeness and idempotent retries.

Validation acceptance matrix — future isolated application tests

  1. Independent decisions

    Changing payable minutes leaves actual/billable unchanged; changing checked billable leaves actual/payable unchanged. Reject negative/over-actual/fractional approved minutes. Null remains different from reviewed zero.

  2. Authority

    NGOCSON succeeds including own time; PM/other admin/member financial changes fail server-side and via direct-field attempts; Daniel's invoice-finalization grant remains distinct. Existing financial access protects cost visibility.

  3. Entry points

    Manual, timer, DayHub, Attendance, Planning confirmed-log path produce consistent proposals. Workday/attendance auto-approval never approves compensation. Budget/cap failures retain actual-time evidence.

  4. Rates

    Work-date boundary, mid-period rate changes, late/backdated logging, overlapping/missing agreements, missing/default rates, genuine zero rate, legacy snapshot provenance and current-rate changes. Non-payable confirmed zero produces zero cost without a rate.

  5. Reconciliation

    Regular/paid rework/unpaid rework/partial/fixed-price/pending/unknown/historical correction; compare every affected parent/child projection, Excel/PDF and screen. Aggregate before explicit presentation rounding; no duplicate cost across invoices or supplier payments.

  6. Invoice variance and attachments

    Checked 5h -> final 7h with reason; reject unexplained variance. Multi-line/entry/period allocations sum to final invoice quantity and net amount; include no freelancer data; archived resend remains unchanged.

  7. Historic and processed records

    Unknown compensation remains unknown; review does not alter original time, issued invoices, payments or old exports. Financial-only adjustment changes compensation/cost only. Reliable actual-time correction remains separately auditable.

  8. Failures/concurrency/cache

    Idempotent retry, stale expected revision, lost acknowledgement, partial bulk success, missing reader fields, failed pagination and cache invalidation. Failed read is unavailable, never a zero-total success.

  9. Currency and release

    Only compare same-currency cost/revenue or an explicitly agreed stored conversion basis. Check declared lint/typecheck/build plus repository report-hour commands after implementation; then read-only production verification after Publish.
