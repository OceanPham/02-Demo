1. Why invoices cannot be sent from Work Packages

The WP package was added but never connected to invoicing. The "Apply billing" action only works for WPs with billing method Hours or Per unit. Fixed price, periodic, milestone, instalment and percentage WPs have no path to an invoice. The invoice editor ignores WPs completely and builds lines from logged hours × hourly price.
